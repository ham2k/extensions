See where your last hour's contacts on the band are, a dot apiece on a map of the world.

## What you get

- **A dot for every contact** you made on your band in the last hour, placed by the station's grid or location, or by its country when that's all the log knows.
- **Newer dots stand out**: each fades as its contact ages, and the latest is drawn larger.
- **Stations still to work**: a smaller dot for each station spotted on your band in the last half hour that you haven't worked yet.
- **Contacts in one place fan out**, so each one shows when the log only knows their state or country.
- **State and ARRL section lines**, and distance rings around your station.
- **In Sweepstakes**, the map frames the ARRL and RAC sections, paints the ones you've worked, tints light red the ones you still need where stations are spotted, and names a section when you rest the pointer on it.
- **A color for each band**, and both radios' bands under SO2R, each with its count of contacts in the hour and of stations spotted.
- **Your band follows your radio**, or the logging fields when no radio is connected.
- **Choose in the panel's settings** the map's style, whether it shows the grayline, and how far apart the rings are.

Scroll or pinch to zoom, and drag to move around the map.
