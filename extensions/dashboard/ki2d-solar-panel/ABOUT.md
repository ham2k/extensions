Check the bands before you call CQ. Add this panel beside your log and see what the sun is doing, and what it means for the band you're on.

## What you get

- **Solar flux, sunspots, A and K** at a glance, with A and K colored when they spell trouble.
- **Day and night conditions** for every HF band.
- **MUF and foF2** from the ionosonde nearest your grid. The MUF turns red when your band is above it; foF2 turns green when your band is below it, so you know close-in NVIS contacts are on.
- **History charts** for solar flux, solar wind and Kp, over one, three or seven days. Slide across them to read any moment.

Turn off what you don't need, pick a theme, and add the panel more than once to keep different views side by side. Set a grid in your operation to get the MUF line.

Data from [N0NBH at hamqsl.com](https://www.hamqsl.com/solar.html), [NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center](https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/) and the ionosondes gathered by [KC2G](https://prop.kc2g.com/stations/).
