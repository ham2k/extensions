Watch your rate while you run: how many contacts an hour you're making right now, and how that's gone across the operation.

## What you get

- **Your 10-minute and 60-minute rates**, charted over time.
- **A goal line** at the rate you're aiming for.
- **A chart per operator** for a multi-op station.

Pick the span to chart and your goal in the panel's settings.
