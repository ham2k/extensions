See your radio's band scope in a panel: its spectrum and waterfall, with the band plan, the spots on the band and anything else marked on it laid over the picture.

## Tune

Click the spectrum or the waterfall to tune there, or turn the mouse wheel. Click a spot to put its station into the logging fields and tune to it, as a tap in the Spots panel does.

## Look around

Drag along the frequency scale to zoom, and drag the picture to pan. Drag the scale up or down to give the spectrum or the waterfall more room.

## Set it up

The gear on the panel opens its settings: spectrum, waterfall or both, which spots to show and how old, how much of the band plan to draw, and the scope's floor, reference level, averaging and speed.

Needs a radio whose scope Ham2K Logger reads, connected under Station Configuration. The band plan comes from the Band Plan extension.
