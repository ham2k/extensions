See who's on the band, laid out along its frequencies, and tap a spot to work it.

## What you get

- **Every spot on your band**, following your radio or the band you pick.
- **What's worth a call** stands out, as in the Spots panel: something new in green, a new multiplier in orange, stations you've already worked dimmed.
- **Your band tags** (QRM!, NET!) and what other extensions mark on the band, among the spots.
- **Band plan markers** for calling frequencies and the digital watering holes.
- **Your radio's frequency** on the map, and its spectrum and waterfall behind the spots when your radio sends them. Click them to tune there.

Where spots crowd, their calls stack out from the frequency scale, and stations right on top of each other share one label, `K1ABC +3`: point at it to see them all.

## Move around

Drag along the frequency scale to zoom in or out, and drag the picture to move along the band. The button in the corner steps between the whole band, your mode's part of it, and a close look around your radio.

## Work it

Tap a spot and the station goes into your log, and your radio tunes to it. Spots come from the spot sources you have in the app.

Right-click a spot to hide it until the station is spotted again, or one of your band tags to take it down.
