See who's on the band, laid out along its frequencies, and tap a spot to work it.

## What you get

- **Every spot on your band**, following your radio or the band you pick.
- **What's worth a call** stands out: new ones in color, stations you've already worked dimmed.
- **Band plan markers** for calling frequencies and the digital watering holes.
- **Your radio's frequency** on the map, and its waterfall behind the spots when your radio sends one.

## Work it

Tap a spot and the station goes into your log, and your radio tunes to it. Spots come from the spot sources you have in the app.
