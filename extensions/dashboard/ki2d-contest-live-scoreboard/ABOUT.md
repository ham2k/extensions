Your contest score on [Contest Online ScoreBoard](https://contestonlinescore.com), live, and your place among the stations around you.

## What you get

Add the **Live Scoreboard** pane to your contest layout. It shows your category on the contest's scoreboard: your place and as many of the stations above and below you as the pane has room for, with their scores, QSOs and multipliers, refreshed every minute.

Switch **Post Score to Contest Online ScoreBoard** on in the operation's **Integrations** dialog, and while the operation is open it posts your score every 2, 3 or 5 minutes, until six hours after your last contact, whether or not the pane is on screen, with your categories, club and operators taken from your Cabrillo log, and your contacts by band and mode unless you leave them out. The switch is for that operation on this device and starts off, even where an earlier version of this extension posted on its own; its settings button picks the interval. Choose the pane's theme (the app's colors, Light, Dark or Teal) in the pane's own settings. The pane's top right corner says when your score was last posted. If something keeps your score from the board, such as a wrong password, the corner and a notice say so.

## Before you go

Register your callsign on contestonlinescore.com and enter its password under **Settings › Accounts, Services & Data Sources › Contest Online ScoreBoard**.
