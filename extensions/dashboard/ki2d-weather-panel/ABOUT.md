Keep an eye on the sky while you operate. Add this panel beside your log and see the weather where you are, and what the next few hours bring.

## What you get

- **Right now**: temperature, conditions, wind and humidity, and the next sunrise or sunset.
- **The next eight hours**: temperature, chance of rain and conditions, hour by hour.

The panel follows your operation's location, or type another place in its settings.

Weather data from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com).
