Work all 50 states for ARRL's [Worked All States](https://www.arrl.org/was), and keep track of every one you work and confirm.

## What you get

- **A checklist of the 50 states**, by mode and by band.
- **A running count** of the states you've worked and confirmed.
- **A notice as you log** a new state, or one on a new band or mode.
- **Spots marked** when working them would add to your checklist.

LoTW and paper QSLs confirm a contact.

## Before you go

No account needed. Your checklist is built from your own log.
