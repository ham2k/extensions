Add **CWT** to an operation to log a [CWops Test](https://cwops.org/cwops-tests/).
Choose the session and enter your exchange during setup.

Enter a callsign to see name and exchange suggestions from past CWT contacts
and cached call history. Review the suggestions and edit or clear them as needed.
CWT tracks your score and flags new calls and duplicates.

Use **Exports** to save the session as ADIF or Cabrillo.
