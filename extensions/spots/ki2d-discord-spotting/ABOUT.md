Post your self-spots to the Discord channels you choose: your club's, your friends', your own.

## Set it up

In Discord, create a webhook in the channel's settings and copy its URL. Add it in Settings › Spotting › Discord Spotting, one entry per channel. Guard that URL like a password: anyone holding it can post to the channel.

## Choose what goes out

Each channel has its own message template. A new one posts only the spots whose comment starts with QRV or QRT, so the channel hears when you go on and off the air. Edit it to post every spot.
