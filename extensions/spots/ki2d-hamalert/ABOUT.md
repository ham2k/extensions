See your [HamAlert](https://hamalert.org) alerts on the Spots panel, and stop hearing about stations you've already worked.

## What you get

Your HamAlert alerts from the last hour, as spots. Work an alerted station and HamAlert stops alerting you about it for a while.

## Before you go

Enter your HamAlert username and password in the extension's settings. On [hamalert.org](https://hamalert.org/triggers), tick **App** for each trigger you want here.
