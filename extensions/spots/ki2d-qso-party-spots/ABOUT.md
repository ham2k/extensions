Find the stations on the air in a state, regional or Canadian QSO party, and spot yourself, with the [QSO Party Hub](http://qsopartyhub.com) and the [QSO Party APRS Tracker](https://mobiletracker.stateqso.com).

## Hunt

During a party, its spots show up on your Spots panel, each with its county, and the county comes along when you log the contact.

## Spot yourself

Self-spot to the Hub from an operation in a QSO party, with your county.

## Before you go

No account needed. Set up your operation with the party's extension, and set your county there.
