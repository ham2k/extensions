Know where you are on the band. The band plan for your station's country and region: each band's edges, where CW, data and phone go, where your license class may transmit, the FT8 and FT4 windows, and the frequencies known for something, such as calling frequencies, QRP, beacons and nets.

It marks them on the band for every panel that shows one, the Band Scope among them, so the scope's picture carries the band's edges and modes without any setup.
