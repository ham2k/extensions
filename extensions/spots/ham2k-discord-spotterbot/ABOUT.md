Send your self-spots to every Discord server that uses Ham2K's [Spotterbot](https://spotterbot.ham2k.net) and lets you post.

## Spot

Self-spot as usual, and Spotterbot posts it to each server that allows you. Switch any server off in Settings › Spotting › Discord Spotterbot.

## Before you go

Sign in with Discord in the extension's settings. Ask your server's admins to add Spotterbot from [spotterbot.ham2k.net](https://spotterbot.ham2k.net).
