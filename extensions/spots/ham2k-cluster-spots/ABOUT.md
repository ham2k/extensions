Read the DX cluster's spots on your Spots panel, and post your own to it.

## What you get

Every spot your cluster node announces, newest per station, band and mode.

## Spot

Self-spot, or spot a station you just worked. Your spot leads with what you're activating, such as `POTA US-1234`.

## Before you go

Pick a node in Settings › DX Cluster. Connecting clears the spot filters set for your call on that node. The web version can't reach a cluster.
