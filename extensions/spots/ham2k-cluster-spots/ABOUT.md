Read the DX cluster's spots on your Spots panel, and post your own to it.

## What you get

Every spot your cluster node announces, the moment it announces it, newest per station, band and mode. The node's latest spots from before you connected show up right away too.

In Settings › Spotting › Cluster Spots, “Spots heard from” can limit the list to spots posted by stations in one continent. Spots from posting stations whose location cannot be determined remain visible.

## Spot

Self-spot, or spot a station you just worked. Your spot leads with what you're activating, such as `POTA US-1234`.

## Before you go

Pick a node in Settings › DX Cluster. Connecting clears the spot filters set for your call on that node. The web version can't reach a cluster.
