Turn your self-spots into APRS position beacons, so chasers watching [aprs.fi](https://aprs.fi) or a QSO party's [mobile tracker](https://mobiletracker.stateqso.com) see where you are and where to find you on the dial.

## Spot

Self-spot as usual, and a beacon goes out under your call, from your operation's location, with your frequency and what you're activating.

## Before you go

No APRS account needed. Set a grid or location on your operation first.
