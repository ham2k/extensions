Hunt WWFF parks and SiOTA silos in Australia and New Zealand, and spot your own activations, with [ParksnPeaks](https://www.parksnpeaks.org).

## Hunt

WWFF and SiOTA spots from VK, ZL and the rest of Oceania show up on your Spots panel.

## Activate

Put a WWFF or SiOTA reference on your operation and spot yourself to ParksnPeaks. Spot the stations you work, too.

## Before you go

Reading spots needs no account. To post, enter your ParksnPeaks callsign and API key in Settings.
