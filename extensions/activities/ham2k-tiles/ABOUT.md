Activate the grid subsquare you're standing in, or hunt the operators who do, with [Tiles on the Air](https://tilesontheair.com).

## Activate

The app offers the tile you're in, or type any one. Roving? Switch to the next tile as you go. Spot yourself straight from the app, then export your log and upload it on tilesontheair.com.

## Hunt

Find activators in the Tiles spots list and pick one to log it.

## Before you go

To spot yourself, add your Offline Spot Gateway PIN from your Tiles profile in Settings.
