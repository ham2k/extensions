Operate from New Zealand's huts, islands, lakes, parks, lighthouses and volcanoes, or chase the operators who do, with [ZL on the Air](https://ontheair.nz).

## Activate

Pick the place you're at from the ones near you, or search by name or reference. Log your contacts, and the app keeps your tally. Export your log when you're done.

## Chase

Find activators in the ZLOTA spots list and pick one to log it, and export a hunter log.

## Before you go

No account needed. The reference list is kept on your device for offline use and updated regularly.
