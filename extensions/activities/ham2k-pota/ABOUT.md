Operate from parks around the world, or hunt the operators who do, with [Parks on the Air](https://parksontheair.com).

## Activate

Pick the park you're in from the parks near you, or more than one. Log ten contacts or more in a UTC day, then export your logs and upload them to [pota.app](https://pota.app). Spot yourself straight from the app, make park-to-park contacts, and see the park's boundaries on the map.

## Hunt

Find activators in the spots list and pick one to log it. Spot the activators you work, and track every park you've hunted.

## Before you go

No account needed. The park list is kept on your device for offline use and updated regularly.
