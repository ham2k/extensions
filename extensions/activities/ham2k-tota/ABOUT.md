Climb a lookout tower and put it on the air, or hunt the operators who do, with [Towers on the Air](https://wwtota.com).

## Activate

Pick the tower from the ones near you, or search by name or reference. Log thirteen contacts in a UTC day, spot yourself straight from the app, and export your log.

## Hunt

Find activators in the TOTA spots list and pick one to log it. Spot the activators you work, and export a hunter log.

## Before you go

No account needed. The tower list is kept on your device for offline use and updated regularly.
