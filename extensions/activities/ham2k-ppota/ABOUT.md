Activate villages and small rural places, or hunt the operators who do, with [Pueblos y Parajes On The Air](https://ppota.app).

## Activate

Pick the village you're in from the ones near you, or search by name or reference. Log five contacts in a UTC day, spot yourself straight from the app, then export your logs and upload them to ppota.app.

## Hunt

Find activators in the spots list and pick one to log it. Spot the activators you work.

## Before you go

No account needed. The reference list is kept on your device for offline use and updated regularly.
