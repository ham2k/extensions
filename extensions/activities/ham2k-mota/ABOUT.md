Operate from windmills and watermills, or chase the stations who do, with Mills on the Air.

## Activate

Pick the mill you're at from the ones near you, or search by name or reference. The app flags duplicates as you log. Spot yourself on [cqgma.org](https://www.cqgma.org) straight from the app, then export your logs.

## Hunt

Log the mill the other station is at, spot the stations you work, see how many mills you've hunted, and export a hunter log.

## Before you go

No account needed. The mill list is kept on your device for offline use and updated regularly.
