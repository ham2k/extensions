Operate from historic sites, from castles and churches to ruins and monuments, or chase the stations who do, with [History on the Air](https://cqhota.app).

## Activate

Pick the site you're at from the ones near you, or search by name or reference. Log five contacts in a UTC day: the app keeps your tally and flags duplicates. Spot yourself straight from the app, and export your logs to upload to cqhota.app.

## Hunt

Find activators in the spots list and pick one to log it. Spot the activators you work.

## Before you go

To post spots, add your HOTA API key to your HOTA account in the app. The site list is kept on your device for offline use and updated regularly.
