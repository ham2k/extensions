Operate from lighthouses and lightships in Belgium for the BLHA award.

## Activate

Pick the lighthouse you're at from the ones near you, or search by name or reference. Log ten contacts: the app keeps your tally and flags duplicates. Spot yourself on [cqgma.org](https://www.cqgma.org) straight from the app, then export your logs.

## Before you go

No account needed. The lighthouse list is kept on your device for offline use and updated regularly.
