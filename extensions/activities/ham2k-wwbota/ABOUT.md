Operate from bunkers around the world, or chase the operators who do, with [Bunkers on the Air](https://wwbota.org).

## Activate

Pick the bunker you're at from the ones near you, or search by name or reference. Log your contacts, and the app keeps your tally. Spot yourself on WWBOTA straight from the app, and export your log.

## Chase

Find activators in the WWBOTA spots list and pick one to log it. Spot the activators you work, and export a hunter log.

## Before you go

No account needed. The bunker list is kept on your device for offline use and updated regularly.
