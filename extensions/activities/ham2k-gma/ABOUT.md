Operate from summits and other sites around the world, or chase the stations who do, with [Global Mountain Activity](https://www.cqgma.org).

## Activate

Pick the summit you're on from the ones near you, or search by name or reference. Spot yourself on cqgma.org straight from the app, then export your log.

## Hunt

Log the summit the other station is on, spot the stations you work, and export a hunter log.

## Before you go

No account needed. The summit list is kept on your device for offline use and updated regularly.
