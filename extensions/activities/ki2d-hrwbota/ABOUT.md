Follow [Ham Radio Workbenches On The Air](https://hrwbota.com) from the logger: see the hosts on the air, keep your score as you work them, and, if you are a host, send your contacts to the event's tracker as you log them.

## Chasing hosts

The hosts on the air show up in the Spots Panel under **HRWBOTA**, with what a contact is worth in each mode.

Add the **HRWBOTA** activity to your operation and, as you type a host's call, the logger says it is a host and what it is worth. Your score follows the event's rules: each host once per mode, at the best value you worked, during the event's hours. The official score is on the tracker, counted from the hosts' logs.

## Hosting

Make a logger key on your host card on the tracker, and enter it under **Settings › Accounts › HRWBOTA**. The key field appears there once your profile's callsign is on the host list.

Then add the **HRWBOTA** activity to your operation. Each contact you log goes to the tracker within a few seconds, and so does your status: on the air, on that frequency and mode, at your operation's parks or summits. Fixing or deleting a contact updates the tracker too. A self-spot also goes to the tracker as your status, and one whose comment starts with QRT takes you off the air.

Contacts go out only from an operation under the call your key belongs to, since the tracker counts everything a key sends for that host. If the tracker can't be reached, your contacts wait and go out when it answers, as long as the logger stays open; it tells you if that goes on for a while.

Your operation's score shows what your log gives your listeners: your contacts, the points they earn by the event's rules, and how many listeners earned them, by mode.
