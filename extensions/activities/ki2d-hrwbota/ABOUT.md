Follow [Ham Radio Workbenches On The Air](https://hrwbota.com) from the logger: see the hosts on the air, and keep your score as you work them.

The hosts on the air show up in the Spots Panel under **HRWBOTA**, with what a contact is worth in each mode.

Add the **HRWBOTA** activity to your operation and, as you type a host's call, the logger says it is a host and what it is worth. Your score follows the event's rules: each host once per band and mode, at the best value you worked, during the event's hours (DMR, AllStar and the other linked networks once per host). The official score is on the tracker, counted from the hosts' logs.

The activity's setup asks for your exchange once: how many soldering irons you have, your favourite one's brand, and your state. The logging row shows it beside the field for the serial number each host sends you.
