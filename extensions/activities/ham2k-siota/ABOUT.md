Activate Australia's grain silos, or chase the operators who do, with [Silos on the Air](https://www.silosontheair.com).

## Activate

Pick the silo from the ones near you, or search by name or code. Log ten contacts to activate it, then export your log.

## Chase

Add the silo to any contact with a station at one, and export a hunter log of the silos you worked.

## Before you go

No account needed. The silo list is kept on your device for offline use and updated regularly.
