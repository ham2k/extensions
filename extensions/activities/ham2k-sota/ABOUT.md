Climb summits around the world and put them on the air, or chase the operators who do, with [Summits on the Air](https://www.sota.org.uk).

## Activate

Pick the summit you're on from the summits near you, or search by name or code, and see its activation zone on the map. Log four contacts or more, then export your log and upload it to the [SOTA database](https://www.sotadata.org.uk). Spot yourself on SOTAwatch straight from the app.

## Chase

Find activators in the SOTAwatch spots list and pick one to log it. Spot the activators you work, and export a chaser log.

## Before you go

Connect your SOTA account in Settings to post spots. The summit list is kept on your device for offline use and updated regularly.
