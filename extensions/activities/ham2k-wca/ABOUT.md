Operate from castles and fortresses around the world with the [World Castles Awards](https://wcagroup.org).

## Activate

Pick the castle from the ones near you, or search by name or reference. Log fifty contacts to activate it, and export your log. Spot yourself on [cqgma.org](https://www.cqgma.org) straight from the app.

## Before you go

No account needed. The castle list is kept on your device for offline use and updated regularly.
