Operate from lakes and lagoons around the world, or hunt the operators who do, with [Lakes and Lagoons on the Air](https://llota.app).

## Activate

Pick the lake you're at from the ones near you, or search by name or reference. Log ten contacts or more in a UTC day, spot yourself straight from the app, make lake-to-lake contacts, and export your logs.

## Hunt

Find activators in the spots list and pick one to log it. Spot the activators you work, see how many lakes you've hunted, and export a hunter log.

## Before you go

No account needed. The lake list is kept on your device for offline use and updated regularly.
