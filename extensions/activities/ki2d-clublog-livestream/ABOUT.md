Your [Club Log](https://clublog.org) Live Stream follows the operation: each contact goes to your Club Log log as you log it.

## What you get

Add the **Club Log Livestream** activity to an operation, and every contact you log in it is sent to Club Log within about half a minute. Fixing a contact sends it again. Contacts logged while you are offline go out once you are back.

## Before you go

Make an API token on clublog.org under **Settings → API**, and enter it under **Settings → Accounts → Club Log**. To show the stream, turn it on under **Settings → Live Stream** on clublog.org.

Club Log takes about a thousand uploads a day from one account, so in a long, busy operation contacts are sent in larger batches as the day goes on.

A contact you delete stays on Club Log, and so does the old version of one whose call, time, band or mode you change: remove those on clublog.org.
