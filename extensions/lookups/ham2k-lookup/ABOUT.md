Look up the calls you log for free, with no account, from Ham2K's own lookup service. It covers stations in the US, Canada, Germany and Norway.

## What you get

- **Name and location**, with a grid square for US and Canadian calls.
- **License class**, where the service knows it.

## Before you go

It needs an internet connection. If you also use QRZ.com, QRZ answers first and this fills in what it leaves out.
