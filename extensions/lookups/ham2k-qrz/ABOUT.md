Look up every call you log on [QRZ.com](https://www.qrz.com), with your own QRZ account.

## What you get

- **Who they are**: name and profile photo.
- **Where they are**: address, grid square and zones.
- **How to reach them**: email and QSL manager.

## Before you go

Enter your QRZ login (your callsign, not your email) and password in the app's Accounts settings. Lookups need an internet connection.
