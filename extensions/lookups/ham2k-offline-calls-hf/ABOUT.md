Look up the calls you log with no internet connection, from a copy of Ham2K's lookup service kept on your device. It covers the stations with HF privileges in the US, Canada, Germany and Norway: about 560,000 of them.

## What you get

- **Name and location**, with a grid square for US and Canadian calls.
- **License class**, where the service knows it.

## Which stations

- **US**: General, Advanced and Amateur Extra, and club stations.
- **Canada**: Basic with Honours, Basic with a Morse qualification, and Advanced.
- **Germany**: Class A and Class E.
- **Norway**: every station.

Technicians, Canada's plain Basic and Germany's Class N are left out. For those calls, Ham2K Lookup Service and QRZ.com still answer while you are online.

## Storage

It needs about **125 MB** on your device. The first download is about 14 MB, then a small update each day brings only the calls that changed.

If you want every public callsign instead, install **Offline Lookups: All Callsigns**. It holds all of these too, so you don't need both.
