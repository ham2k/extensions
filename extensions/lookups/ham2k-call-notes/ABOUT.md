Know who's on the other end. Type a call and its note shows beside it: who they are, their club, the friend you promised a sked.

## What you get

- **Ham2K's [Hams of Note](https://ham2k.com/data/hams-of-note.txt)**, built in.
- **Your own notes files**, as many as you like.

## Make your own

Write a plain text file, one call per line followed by its note, and share it from Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud Drive, a GitHub Gist or any link. Add it in Settings, under Lookups. Your notes files are kept on your device for offline use and updated regularly.
