Look up the calls you log with no internet connection, from a copy of Ham2K's lookup service kept on your device. It covers every public callsign in the US, Canada, Germany and Norway: about 980,000 stations.

## What you get

- **Name and location**, with a grid square for US and Canadian calls.
- **License class**, where the service knows it.

## Storage

It needs about **225 MB** on your device. The first download is about 24 MB, then a small update each day brings only the calls that changed.

To save space, **Offline Lookups: HF Callsigns** holds only the stations with HF privileges, in about 125 MB. This one includes all of those, so you don't need both.
