Your [Club Log](https://clublog.org) Live Stream follows the operation: each contact goes to your Club Log log as you log it.

## What you get

Switch **Club Log Livestream** on in an operation's **Integrations** dialog, and every contact you log in it is sent to Club Log within about half a minute. Fixing a contact sends it again, and deleting one deletes it on Club Log. Contacts logged while you are offline go out once you are back. Under the switch, the dialog names the call your contacts go out as, or says what keeps them from going out.

The switch is for that operation on this device. In an operation logged on several devices, switch it on in just one: contacts from the others reach it through sync and stream from there.

## Before you go

Make an API token on clublog.org under **Settings → API**, and enter it under **Settings → Accounts → Club Log**. To show the stream, turn it on under **Settings → Live Stream** on clublog.org.

Club Log takes about a thousand uploads a day from one account, so in a long, busy operation contacts are sent in larger batches as the day goes on.

When the app restarts its extensions, it forgets which Club Log contact each of yours became. Deleting a contact sent before that, or changing its call, time, band or mode, leaves the old one on Club Log: remove those on clublog.org.

An operation that had the Club Log Livestream activity from an earlier version keeps its name, but streams only once you switch it on in Integrations.
