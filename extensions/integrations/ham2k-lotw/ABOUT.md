Upload your contacts to ARRL's [Logbook of the World](https://lotw.arrl.org), signed with the certificates you use in TQSL.

## What you get

- **Push to LoTW** under Exports, from an operation, Chasing or your Logs: every contact not yet sent, for the callsign of one of your certificates.
- **Up to three certificates**, so each of your callsigns uploads with its own.
- **A certificate check** when you test the account: its callsign, its dates, and whether LoTW has revoked or replaced it.

## Before you go

In TQSL, save each callsign certificate as a `.p12` file ([ARRL's guide](https://lotw.arrl.org/lotw-help/save-certificate/)). Then in the app, open Settings → Exports & Imports → Services → Logbook of the World, choose each file, and enter its password if you set one. The files stay on this device.

Uploads need the desktop or mobile app and an internet connection. LoTW queues what you send; its processing result is under Your Account → Your Activity in LoTW.
